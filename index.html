Why is it crucial to maintain or upgrade your landscape?

Your garden is your property’s first impression, standing up to Perth’s harsh sun, sandy soil, and dry summers. Over time, even the best-designed landscapes can show signs of wear. Common issues include:

Overgrown or dying lawns Poor drainage & waterlogging Cracked paving or retaining walls Outdated garden design

Ignoring these problems can lead to costly repairs and reduced street appeal. A professional landscape upgrade not only restores your outdoor space but also improves water efficiency and long-term property value.

Frequently asked questions

We understand that choosing the right landscaping solution comes with questions. Below are some of the most common queries we receive to help you make an informed decision with confidence.

How long does it take for a new lawn to establish?

Most turf takes 2–3 weeks to establish root systems with proper watering, while a lawn grown from seed can take 6–8 weeks. We’ll advise on the best option for Perth’s climate and your soil type.

Is landscaping disruptive to my property during installation?

We plan every project to minimise disruption, working in stages where needed and keeping access clear. Most residential jobs are completed with minimal impact on your daily routine.

Can you redesign my garden around existing trees or features?

Absolutely. We always assess existing trees, structures, and features first, and our team will recommend the best approach to incorporate or protect them in your new design.

How long does a full landscaping project take?

Most residential landscaping projects are completed within one to three weeks, depending on the size and complexity of the design and weather conditions.

Is artificial turf a low-maintenance option?

Yes. Artificial turf requires no mowing or watering and holds up well to Perth’s dry summers, making it a popular choice for busy households and water-wise gardens.

Do gardens require a lot of maintenance after installation?

We design with low-maintenance, water-wise planting in mind. A simple regular watering and seasonal tidy-up is usually all that’s needed to keep your garden performing at its best for years.
